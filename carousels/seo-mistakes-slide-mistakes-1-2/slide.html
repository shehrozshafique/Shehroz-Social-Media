Mistake 1: One page trying to rank for everything.

Check: is your homepage targeting every service?

Fix: one service = one page, each with its own title, H1 and FAQs.

Mistake 2: Titles nobody clicks.

Check: Search Console > Performance > Pages, sort by impressions. High impressions with CTR under 2% = weak title.

Fix: Service + Town + Reason to click. Example: "Emergency Plumber Leeds | 60-Minute Callout".
