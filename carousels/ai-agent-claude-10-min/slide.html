How to Build
AI Agent with Claude
in 10 Minutes

No coding. No complex setup. Just Claude + a few simple steps.
10 MIN
STEP 1

Go to Claude (claude.ai)

Open your browser and visit claude.ai. Sign in to your account (or create one if you're new).

STEP 2

Use this prompt

"I want to build an AI agent. Ask me the right questions one by one to set it up. I'm new to this, so keep it simple and beginner-friendly."
STEP 3

Add a skill (optional but powerful)

In Settings, turn on useful capabilities like Skills, Web search and Code execution. This helps your agent do more.

STEP 4

Connect your tools

Go to Connectors and add the tools you need: Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Slack and more. Just click, authorize and connect.

STEP 5

Set up your agent

Create a Project: give it a name, a description and instructions that define its goal.

"Help me manage my emails and summarize important messages daily."
STEP 6

Configure settings

Adjust the behavior, response style and permissions. Choose how it should respond, which tools to use and what it can access.

STEP 7

Test your agent

Try a few real tasks. Ask it something like:

"Search for the latest AI news and give me a 3-point summary."
STEP 8

Make it live

If everything works, keep it in your Project. Use it anytime on the web, desktop or mobile app.

STEP 9

Improve over time

See what works, tweak the prompt, add more skills or tools. The more you use it, the smarter it gets.

STEP 10

Enjoy your AI agent!

You just built your first AI agent with Claude in 10 minutes. Now go build something amazing!
